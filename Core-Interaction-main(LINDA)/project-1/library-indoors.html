A Little Escape in Seward Park

Book-Filled Nostalgia

probably because I was so much smaller as well. Curious about what new books they had, I picked
one out and sat down at one of the reading tables to read.

A few minutes later, my friend texted saying she's almost at her final stop.
I should head over and meet her there.

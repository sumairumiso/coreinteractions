How to Make a Felt Doll!

Main Body

  1. Sketch out a design of the character you want to make.
  2. Draw and cut a stencil of the body.
  3. Trace the reference onto a piece of felt. Cut around the shapes. Make sure to have 2 pieces for the front and back.
  4. Draw and cut out the stencil of the eyes and pupils.
  5. Using the stencil, trace out the shape of the eyes onto another color felt of your choice. Cut it out.
  6. Sew the details of the eyes together before placing it on the front side of the body.
  7. Sew the eyes onto the body using a straight stitch or back stitch.
  8. Line both sides of the body together. Sew the edges together using a blanket stitch.
  9. Leave a hole to fill the doll with stuffing. Don't be shy to add a lot.
  10. Sew the hole closed so that no stuffing will fall out.
  11. Separate the head and body by sewing a straight stitch across the neck.

Hair

  1. Draw the front and back of the hair using the doll's head as a reference.
  2. Trace around the stencils onto a color felt of your choice.
  3. Cut out the hair pieces. Make sure there's enough fabric around the edges to sew together.
  4. Line up the front and back of the hair and sew the edges together with a blanket stitch. If there is overlapping of felt, use a straight stitch for those areas.

Clothes & Accessories

  1. Trace out clothes on paper using doll's dimensions as a reference.
  2. Cut out felt pieces for the front and back of the clothes.
  3. Experiment placing the pieces on the doll to make sure it fits fine. Keep cutting till you get the perfect shape while having extra edges to sew the pieces together.
  4. Line up the pieces and sew them around the character's body.
  5. Cut a straight line down the back for easy changing in case you want to dress it up with other options.
  6. Add any accessories as you wish (hair clip, earrings etc.)

You're done! Enjoy your cute personal creation!

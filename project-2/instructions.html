"Recipe" for How to Make a Chopstick Rest!

  1. First, take out the chopstick paper covering, making sure to not rip out the top.

  2. Fold the wrapper halfway.

  3. Fold that same piece halway again.

  4. Fold the top corners on both sides.

  5. Fold the bottom corners on both sides as well.

  6. With the corner folds, push in the middle between them to create the stand.

  7. Lastly, gently push down the middle of the paper to create a curved space for the chopstick to rest!

    8. Congrats!

    You have successfully made a chopstick rest out of the wrapper. Now, you can use this cool skill and use it for any restuarant you go to!
